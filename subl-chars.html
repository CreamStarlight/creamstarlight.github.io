Miru

It's me!

Name's Miru Novae here, though in the past I went by another name "Cream Starlight".

If you ask me who I am, in the context of "me being here" I'd say that I'm an accidental wanderer finding home inside the world the storage closet offers. Wanderer as in... suddenly finding yourself in an environment you don't quite know after being hit. By a truck. No, please, don't play that "Asgore Runs Over Dess" near me.

What do I do here? Well, it's mainly... scavenging stuff, going out with people, chatting with them, occasionally helping with their problems, looking for diamond in the deepest abyss, and stuff like this. I need to admit that this might seem plain, but really - it isn't.

We occasionally do exciting or amazing stuff, like going out on an expedition, looking for treasure while avoiding skeletons, building things (I once built a hundred-meter-tall pillar and after managing to getting myself off it I climbed onto its top again), explore unfamiliar places and something. It's not very boring, really.

I'd perceive myself as, like... someone who wants to help. A lot. Sometimes, I would go out a long way to get some kind of material just because a friend needs it for something important. ...And because of this, I actually accidentally stumbled upon a place that is SUUUUUPER rich in clay! We do not need to worry about the brick problem anymore.

Though, I also would think I've been having a little abnormal temper. You see, that arson incident really troubled me. It still brings anger to me whenever it comes into my mind. ...I suppose that's reasonable though. That guy will not be forgiven, period.

...You're curious about my heart's color? Wait. You must have gotten something wrong. Here's the thing -

The heart of a human is a vital organ to them that beats, gives off comforting sound, and runs blood around their body to keep themself alive. Taking it out without some tubes for extending blood vessels is almost ALWAYS an effective method of killing someone. That, and also, certainly I can't just slash my ribcage open and show mine to you? And even if I manage to do so I think you'd find nothing special about mine. I'm just a regular human girl, after all.

You must have been referring to another heart-shaped thing called a soul, which contains one's mind, spirit, memory, personality, vitality, such - I think it's sort of connected to the brain but I have no evidence - and is usually somehow located in the same place as one's heart. Though, using the right method, you can actually remove yours from yourself with ease. Just... don't go hard on it (AND I MEAN IT) or else you yourself might also get hurt. (Clutching it hard effectively gives you manual cardiac arrest and severe chest discomfort until you release it. I've tried it out of ignorance. Don't.) It's made with soft substance that has entanglements to your physical and mental feelings, not crystal that is very hard to break. ...I think I wandered too far from the topic? Anyway, mine's sky blue. I sometimes take it out to look at it. Don't mind me, just a weird quirk.

...

And someone once asked me if I'm looking for a boyfriend. My answer: I am taken. The question is cringe but I decided to put it here anyway.

...

If you ever find me in a bad condition yet am pretending I'm not, try forcing me into looking at myself thoroughly & give me treatments. I'd appreciate it if you do, because I'm usually not aware of how hurt I truly am.

* ...Please keep yourself safe, kid.

Stag RIP 2026.08~2026.09

It's... been a while since I last see him.

He's a friendly deer who introduced several people to Forest Sanctuary!

I also have met him, and we've gone on an expedition and a netherite search. It's a bit eventful!

The expedition also had Jack, and we arrived at a castle before going back. I think he educated Jack on the history of this place afterwards.

The netherite search also had Will, and we'd gone out a long way. We found a bastion, and I got a netherite ingot and a magical diamond pick with no problem. ...Will had a bit of a bad luck there, only getting one netherite scrap.

But afterwards...?

...I don't see him anywhere anymore. This is concerning...

Jack

I think I know his name in full? Something like Jack D. Beaumont?

This kid is... surprisingly inoffensive. Maybe too inoffensive that it becomes offensive in some way.

Last time we fought mob together, he only gave a weak punch on a zombie. I don't remember seeing that zombie even flinch.

But then, I've heard rumors where he... flirts with zombies and skeletons. And then they get so confused they somehow hit themselves, and end up killing themselves.

...I still don't know who exactly he is, outside of being a helpful member of reconstructing our Sanctuary and kind to everyone around.
