Well, if you never have known Phigros, I can give a brief description here:

Geopelia & Gino adventuring in a subliminal <strong>Pixel Tower</strong> witnessing its rise and fall, eventually escaping it returning to the <strong>\[\[True Home, True World\]\]</strong> and meeting again.

I only played this game like, seldomly. I didn't have a lot of time to play it, and neither was I very hyped to play it. I only got a hang of how to play it and some of its lore and still proceeded.

The previous chapters haven't interested me much, as they are mostly just Geopelia dealing with stuff and only requires minimum puzzle-solving.

Then, here comes Chapter 8.

Apparently, the Pixel Tower is slowly being eaten away by a blackhole. A lot of the people aren't aware of it outside of some specific people. Now the event horizon has arrived at the (virtual) Moon.

When you've seen the fireworks of luminescence, your fate starts distorting from others. For those who doesn't, what awaits is destruction in 3, 2, 1. Geopelia was one of those who'd seen the luminescence.

After what seemed to be the end, Geopelia and Gino both woke up in the Zenith, and there it goes. Chapter 9.

They be trying to find a way out.

... I think I might be doing spoilers too much. Just... go do it yourself.

I don't know much about the lore, but I'm glad to see that when they managed to escape the Pixel Tower, they managed to meet each other again in the Reality.
