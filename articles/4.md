So yeah, this article is meant to tell you about my everyday life in the Fuzhou University.

There might be some inaccuracies though as I um. Am a bit too lazy to take part in various activities.

I've been doing well in the university, so don't worry too much about me. In fact, you can sort of say I'm having fun with it. It has the shops, the sceneries, the trees, the food, the environment, and the goats (YES I'M TOTALLY SRS WITH THIS ONE).

So yeah, here's it: The first term here is relatively free. Free as in "You have a lot of time and can do whatever you want".

---

## Why "have a lot of time"?

We actually take breaks on Saturdays and Sundays, and our homework ain't too much to handle. Further, "time limit" isn't really a thing when you get back to dorm at 9:00 pm and get 2~3 whole hours of doing whatever you want.

Sometime, when a vacation arrives, you will suddenly have PLENTIFUL of time. You can use this time to further improve yourself (...academic inflation is caused by those people of your kind of you do this), read a few books, master a skill, or even go out for the scenery or have a trip out! I did every of them during my seven-day break granted by the National Day.

We do need to worry about the mid-term and final exams though, so here's that: if you aren't confident enough, take some time doing researches in what you learn, and when you're confused over something, you can ask somebody about it.

## Why "can do whatever you want"?

Well, to be frank, the major I'm in is... kinda special. I'm in the IT fields, and therefore can bring my laptop to the evening study event for developing things. You may say that, if I wanted, I could have played Minecraft in the classroom! I take most of the time developing my own website and browsing irrelevant stuff though.

But outside of that, freshman is REALLY in general free. As long as you don't do something that can get you punished, you're pretty much free around here. Wanna have some soft drinks? Deal. Wanna have a sip of tea milk? Yup. In need of another key because you've lost one? Roger that. Heck, you can even play card games and run DnD campaigns in the cafeteria. It's shocking we have this much freedom at college.

---

So yeah, the college is pretty free early on. I don't know what it'd look like afterwards though, but I'd imagine things getting harder. As I adapt to the place more, I might share more on it.

However, still, I need to mind you about some of the things.

When you are free like this, you tend to forget what you should do and get so indulged in having fun you're disserving your health. Here's it: keep a note of what you should be doing, and if necessary, get a timetable and try to do things according to it. Don't stay up too late as well, as this can disrupt the clock inside you at best, and keeps your head foggy for a whopping 12 HOURS AFTER YOU WAKE UP AT WORST. Don't do that, trust.
